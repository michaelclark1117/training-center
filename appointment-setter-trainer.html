Appointment Setting Script

    
Word for word. Two-time close. Read it back.

    
1. Opener (reach the owner)

    
"Hi, is the owner or manager available?"

    
If a gatekeeper asks what it's regarding:

    
"It's about the charges you pay for accepting Visa and Mastercard. Who handles that for the business?"

    
Get the decision-maker's name even if they aren't available:

    
"What's their first name, and when's the best time to catch them?"

    
2. The Pitch

    
"Hi [Prospect Name], this is [Your Name], I'm calling with HPD. The reason for my call is because there's been a change. Now, anytime you accept Visa or Mastercard for payment, the charges you pay can be reduced down to zero.

Now, we don't want you to do anything right now. We'll have one of our managers give you a call and explain how you can reduce your charges down to zero and give you all the details. There's no obligation.

Can they call you tomorrow at 10 AM, or would 2 PM be a better time?"

    
3. Lock the Time

    
Once they pick a time, don't celebrate or add more pitch. Go straight to confirming.

    
"Perfect, [10 AM / 2 PM] tomorrow. Let me just get a couple quick details so they're prepared when they call."

    
4. Quick Qualifiers

    
Conversational, not an interrogation.

    

      
Current Processor"Who do you process with right now, is it Square, Clover, your bank?"

      
Volume"Roughly how much do you run in cards a month? Ballpark is fine, under 10,000, 10 to 30, more than that?"

      
Confirms decision-maker"And you're the one who makes the call on the processing, correct?"

      
Email"What's the best email to send a confirmation to?"

    

    
The handoff sheet also needs the decision-maker's last name. Get it before you hang up.

    
5. Confirm & Close

    
"Great. So [Rep Name] will call you at [time] tomorrow at [phone number], is that the best number? Their name is [Rep Name], so keep an eye out for the call. Thanks, [Prospect Name], have a great day."

    
Read back the phone number and time. That's the single biggest driver of show rate.

    
6. Common Objections

    
Rule of thumb: acknowledge, give one short line, and go back to the two-time close. Two attempts max, then thank them and move on.

    

      
"What's the catch?" / "How is it zero?""Great question, and that's exactly what the manager will walk you through. It only takes a few minutes. Does 10 or 2 work better?"

      
"I'm happy with my processor.""Totally understand, most people we talk to are. This isn't about switching today. It's just to show you how the charges can go to zero, so you can compare. Would tomorrow at 10 or 2 be better?"

      
"Just send me something.""I can, but every business is set up a little differently, so it's a lot quicker for the manager to spend five minutes with you than to guess in an email. Morning or afternoon better?"

      
"I'm busy.""That's exactly why we set a time. It's quick. Would early morning or later afternoon be easier?"

      
"I'm under contract.""No problem, the manager can look at that too. A lot of times it still makes sense. 10 or 2 tomorrow?"

    

    
Disqualifiers (don't submit)

    
No decision-maker confirmation · Monthly volume below  · Business doesn't currently take cards
