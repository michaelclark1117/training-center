The call

  1. Open. Give the reason for the call, ask for ten minutes, and get a "Fair enough?"
  2. Diagnose. Ask open questions ("Walk me through...") and say "Tell me more about that." Talk less than half the call.
  3. Quantify. Get the merchant to say their yearly fee out loud.
  4. Reframe. Turn the fee into a choice they can stop making, framed as a yearly loss.
  5. Show the plan. Bring up the customer fear before they do: signage, receipts with both prices, a staff line, a 30-day check-in.
  6. Close. Recap, ask for the legal name and Tax ID, then stop talking.
