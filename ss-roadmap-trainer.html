The roadmap

  1. The Pitch. Who we are and why you're calling: "Hi [Name], this is [Rep] with Harlow Payments. We're a merchant services company out of Plano, and we work with businesses all over the country. I'm calling about our Transact for Free program, where we eliminate your credit card processing fees." Then explain how it works (a small technology fee on card payments covers the cost: a $30 sale on a card rings up $31.20), and go straight to "Have you ever switched processors before?"
  2. The Investigation. Ask open-ended questions only. Start with "Have you ever switched processors before?" Don't pitch.
  3. The Close. Once you have all 7 must-know answers, recap their reason and need, give the savings in dollars, deliver the 8-to-5 care promise, then ask: "What's your Tax ID?"
